# Episode 7: Restyle in one instruction

Length: 10 minutes. Recorded as one real session, with the waiting cut.

## Goal

One instruction changes the whole look of the kickoff deck, live deck and PDF alike, figures and clip included. The second half shows the judgement that remains: a palette made for white paper does not survive inversion unchanged.

## What the viewer sees

The instruction typed. The assistant writes `_brand-dark.yml` and `_quarto-dark.yml`, builds the dark profile into `_build-dark/`, runs the contrast check, re-renders the clip, and shoots every slide. The paper and dark PNGs side by side: the figures came along by themselves, because they name `nmSlide` and tint towards it. Then the failures: the amber of the DoG model and the magenta of the CSS model lose their separation on black; the light-grey stimulus apertures of the clip become dark-grey patches. The assistant proposes tints, the contrast tool confirms them, one figure is judged by eye, and the assistant says which fixed images were regenerated by their scripts and which stay.

## The prompt

```text
Restyle the whole kickoff deck: black background, light-purple titles and
eyebrows, green body text with green-tinted white for emphasis, Roboto for
everything, headings included. Keep the role colours distinguishable on
black, keep the figures and the clip consistent with the new palette, and
show me every slide when you are done.
```

## Commands the assistant runs

```sh
lk build examples/kickoff --profile dark
lk review contrast examples/kickoff/_brand-dark.yml --mode dark
lk clips examples/kickoff --only bar-bold --mode dark
lk review shoot examples/kickoff/_build-dark/slides-live.html --out examples/kickoff/review/dark --sheet
lk review shoot examples/kickoff/_build/slides-live.html --out examples/kickoff/review/paper --sheet
```

## Points to make

- The style is defined once, in `_brand.yml`; every colour is a token in LaTeX, CSS and Python (`docs/THEMING.md`), and the fonts are bundled, so Roboto is a name in the brand file and nothing else.
- The TikZ figures recompile by themselves: their source names tokens, the tokens are part of the cache key, and no figure uses `white` or a `col!pct` tint, which would stay a light card on black.
- `heading` and `body` are optional tokens; with them, `ink` becomes the emphasis colour: light-purple titles, green text, green-tinted white emphasis.
- Roboto has no math companion: mathematics stays in TeX Gyre Pagella Math.
- The clip is the known limit of the one-instruction restyle: a rendered video is pixels. `lk clips --mode dark` renders it again on the dark brand into `assets/clips/dark/`, a minute or two of rendering, and a dark build plays that copy for a movie named with `clip=` (`docs/CLIPS.md`). The kickoff's slide 1 still names its file with `src=`, so its dark build plays the paper clip until the slide moves to `clip=`.
- The last ten percent is taste, checked by a tool and decided by a person.

## Artefacts

- `before/_brand.yml`: the paper preset.
- `after/_brand-dark.first.yml`: the assistant's first version; `after/_brand-dark.yml`: after the palette judgement.
- `after/contrast-first.txt` and `after/contrast-final.txt`: the contrast check before and after.
- `after/paper/slide-NN.png` and `after/dark/slide-NN.png`: every slide in both presets, and the two contact sheets.
