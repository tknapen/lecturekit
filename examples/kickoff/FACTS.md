# Facts on the slides and where they come from

Checked 4 November 2026 unless stated. A claim that is not here is not on a slide.


- Proposal (OEC 2024 full proposal, 30 May 2024): title "Popylar: Popularizing Population Receptive Field Modeling in Cognitive Neuroscience"; lead applicant Knapen; co-applicants Cornelissen, Dumoulin, Groen, Haak, Harvey, Petridou; requested about two person-years of eScience Center research software engineering; indicative start 1 January 2025, end 1 July 2027; 29 letters of support; 1000+ publications and 100+ labs; the eleven-package table (mrVista, prfpy, braincoder, analyzePRF, SamSrf, mrTools, popeye, AFNI, BrainVoyager, VSNLabTools; mrVista, prfpy and braincoder named as direct inspirations); the objectives (multi-backend engine, test-driven development, parameter recovery, governance, development committee, Brain Hacks, benchmark datasets, tutorial notebooks, yearly workshop).
- prfmodel: PyPI releases 0.1.0 on 27 October 2026 and 0.2.0 on 3 November 2026; Apache-2.0; Python 3.10 to 3.13; Keras with TensorFlow, PyTorch or JAX; README credits braincoder for the fitting workflow. API (0.2.0 docs): Gaussian1D/2D, CSS, DoG, DivNorm, DelayedNorm, CenterSurround, CST, GaussianCF, CSF models; GridFitter, LeastSquaresFitter, SGDFitter; impulse responses TwoGamma, DerivativeTwoGamma, ShiftedGamma, Sustained, Transient; six tutorials, four examples (visual fMRI, numerosity fMRI, ECoG model comparison, connective field).
- GitHub contributors API, 4 November 2026: maltelueken 389, angel-daza 79, f-hafner 22, KiranMauro 1. Commit history: connective field stimulus and model December 2025; DoG model and multi-branch docs February 2026; DoG tutorial online March 2026.
- Workshop site: schedule (provisional) and the seven practical notebooks.
- popeye: DeSimone, Rokem and Schneider, JOSS 2016, doi 10.21105/joss.00103.
