# Storyboard

The brief as given on 4 November 2026 (the lecturer's words, condensed), and the shape that came out of it.

> For the workshop I need to create an opening presentation of about 15 minutes, 5 to 7 slides. Use the attached
> proposal as a guide: where the effort came from, what we intended in architecture and community, where prfmodel
> stands. Highlight the awesome work that Malte Lüken has done. prfmodel descends from popeye, prfpy and braincoder.
> A beautiful and slick kickoff presentation.

The audience: the workshop's participants on the first morning, most of whom fit pRFs already and have not read the
proposal. The one thing to take away: prfmodel is community software that came out of a funded plan, is built by a
named engineer on an architecture that lets a model be composed from parts, and is theirs to extend this week.

The argument in order: why pRF mapping deserves one engine (a clip of the method, the size of the field); whose
shoulders the package stands on (the lineage, with credit to the authors of the earlier packages); what the proposal
asked for, in two halves, the engine and the community; where the package stands at 0.2.0 (the shelf of models, with
room for yours); the person who built it (Malte Lüken, with the timeline and the commit counts beside the names of
the other contributors); the two days ahead (the programme as a figure).

Moments that need a visual: the method moving (slide 1, the only clip); the family tree; the engine as a staged
pipeline that appears part by part; the model shelf with an empty card that appears on a key press; the calendar.

Tone: warm, brief, factual; every number from the proposal, PyPI, GitHub or the workshop site; names in type, no logos.
